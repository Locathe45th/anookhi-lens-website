About the
Competition

A global platform dedicated to celebrating visual storytelling, artistic excellence, and cultural diversity through photography — held annually to commemorate World Photography Day.

Who Can Participate

  • Open to photographers worldwide — amateur and professional
  • No age restriction; students and enthusiasts welcome
  • Individual entries only, one submission per category

Requirements

  • Categories: Nature & Wildlife, Open Monochrome, Travel & Street
  • Original, unpublished work owned by the entrant
  • Minimum resolution and file-format guidelines apply

Limitations

  • Entries close strictly at the stated deadline
  • Excessive digital manipulation is not permitted
  • Full terms are detailed in the official rulebook

Our Story

About
Anookhi Lens

[Placeholder copy] Anookhi Lens was founded on a simple belief — that every frame holds a story worth telling, and every storyteller deserves a stage. What began as a small collective of photography enthusiasts has grown into a global community connecting visual artists across borders, cultures, and disciplines.

[Placeholder copy] Our founder set out to build more than a contest — a movement that celebrates authenticity over algorithms, and craft over clout. Her goal is to give emerging photographers the same spotlight as established names, and to make world-class recognition accessible to anyone with a lens and a point of view.

[Placeholder copy] Today, Anookhi Lens continues to expand its reach — partnering with institutions, mentors, and brands who share the same vision: a world that sees itself more clearly, one photograph at a time.
